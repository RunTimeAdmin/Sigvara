Score epochs

A Sigvara score is never written in one step. Each epoch the oracle proposes a score, together with a Merkle root of the payments behind it. The proposal then sits in a challenge window: a second operator recomputes it, and the committee can reject it. Once the window closes, anyone can finalize it. This page shows each of those steps for one agent.
