Simulation

Testnet simulation

Sigvara is running a small, simulated agent economy on Arc testnet so anyone can watch its scoring and slashing work. Every agent and customer on this page is operated by Sigvara. None of it is real demand, and none of it should be read as traction.

Timeline

What happens when, over the four weeks.

The cast

Twenty simulated agents in four kinds. Each kind behaves differently, so its score should end up somewhere different.

What to look for

  • Steady agents climb. They deliver almost every job, so their scores should rise week by week. A score matures slowly, so the score other contracts read trails the earned one.
  • Failing agents stall. Testnet records only successful outcomes, so a failing agent shows up as fewer signed outcomes and customers drifting away, not as a list of failures.
  • The ring does not buy a high score. Its customers are wallets its own operator funds. Caps on what one payer can add should limit what that money buys, and two ring agents are slashed.
  • Every number can be checked. Open an agent on the Agents page and press Check evidence to recompute its score's evidence yourself.

How it works and what it cannot show: docs/simulation.md. Every address it controls: simulation.json.
