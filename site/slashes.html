Slashes

A slash takes an agent's stake. The slashing committee files it with an evidence hash and a victim, the agent is suspended at once, and the operator has a challenge window to dispute it. An undisputed slash can then be executed by anyone: 75% of the stake is burned and 25% goes to the victim. The committee is paid nothing.

Arc testnet (5042002). Reads only. Proceedings come from the Arc explorer's index of the staking and bond contracts; the evidence from the transaction that filed each slash.
