Privacy

Sigvara has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. This page lists what is recorded when you use this site and the public Sigvara services, and for how long.

Last updated 22 November 2026.

This site

sigvara.xyz sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking. Its scripts, styles and fonts are all served from sigvara.xyz itself.

Pages that show live data keep recent answers in your browser's session storage so they load faster. That storage stays on your device and clears when you close the tab.

The testnet app creates agent keys in your browser. They are never sent to us, so we cannot recover a key you lose.

The web host that serves sigvara.xyz keeps standard request logs (IP address, time, page and browser type) under its own retention policy.

Services your browser contacts

Pages that read the chain, such as Check an agent, the explorer and the testnet app, connect from your browser to Arc's public testnet RPC (rpc.testnet.arc.io) and block explorer (explorer.testnet.arc.io). Others run those services. They see your IP address and what you looked up, under their own policies.

Some pages also call Sigvara's own services at oracle.sigvara.xyz, checker.sigvara.xyz and index.sigvara.xyz, which any program can call too. Their servers keep access logs (IP address, time, request, response and browser type) for up to 30 days, to keep the services running and to stop abuse. Rate limits hold your IP address in memory for an hour at most.

What the protocol records

Agent registrations, bonds, payments and scores are recorded on public blockchains: Arc, and Base for some payments. Anyone can read them, and nobody, including us, can change or delete them. A wallet address is not a name, but it can identify you if it is linked to you somewhere else.

When a payer sends a signed outcome to an oracle, the operator stores it with the payment it refers to, as evidence for the agent's score, and serves it publicly so that other operators can check the same score. Operators keep payment records while they still count toward a score: about two and a half years at today's settings.

Email

If you write to support@sigvara.xyz or security@sigvara.xyz, we use your address and message to answer you, and we do not pass them on without asking you first.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to support@sigvara.xyz. Server logs also expire on their own. Data on a blockchain cannot be deleted by anyone.
