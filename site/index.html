The protocol

Identity is a start.
Accountability is the signal.

Sigvara turns agent behavior into a trust signal that other systems can verify, understand, and act on.

Signed identity

Anchor an agent to a cryptographic identity and let peers verify who is acting before they grant access.

Computed reputation

Combine outcomes, attestations, age, external feedback, and community signals into one readable score.

Stake-backed action

Reputation carries weight when it has consequences. Bonds and challenge windows make bad behavior costly.

A shared trust layer

From agent claim
to useful proof.

Designed for agents, operators, and the products that need to decide whether an autonomous action is safe to trust.
